02 / METHOD

From intake to export.

A straightforward sequence keeps the case record readable.

  1. 01

    Open a case

    Record its scope, investigator, and current status.

  2. 02

    Register evidence

    Store an isolated copy and record its SHA-256 baseline.

  3. 03

    Review artifacts

    Inspect bounded file previews and analyze supported logs.

  4. 04

    Export the record

    Generate a case report and keep its digest in the ledger.

03 / LIMITS & CONTEXT

Built for triage and demonstration.

DFT is an academic prototype. It has no user accounts or role controls. Log matches are investigative leads, not proof of malicious activity. Application-level hashes can detect changed stored files, but cannot protect the database and files from an administrator who can alter both.
